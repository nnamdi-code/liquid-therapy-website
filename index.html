Our Botanicals
Our Botanicals

Not every ingredient has a Nigerian story. But the ones that do — we source them with intention.

Directly from Lagos markets, Nigerian farms, and the growing regions most people have never heard of. Each one has a name, a place, and a season.

Lagos Markets · Dec–Apr

Agbalumo

Found on wheelbarrows across Surulere, Mushin, and Oshodi every December. We buy in volume, preserve carefully, and keep it in the glass year round.

Therapeutic notes: Vitamin C. The taste of a Lagos childhood.

Kaduna State · Year Round

Nigerian Ginger

Among the finest ginger in the world. Grown in Kaduna and exported globally — yet most products carry no Nigerian identity. We keep the origin in every glass.

Therapeutic notes: Anti-inflammatory. A slow burn that arrives exactly when it should.

Kano & Sokoto · Year Round

Hibiscus

Northern Nigeria produces the world's most vibrant hibiscus. We use it as a colour note and tart accent — not the headline. There is more going on underneath.

Therapeutic notes: Rich in antioxidants. Deep ruby colour.

Oyo State · Feb–Jul

Ogbomosho Mango

A different fruit to anything imported. Deeper, less sweet, more complex. Sourced from Oyo traders during peak season, frozen in batches to carry the flavour all year.

Therapeutic notes: Rich in vitamins A and C. A tropical depth no imported mango replicates.

Ondo State · May–Sep

Cocoa Pulp

We use the white fruit around the cocoa bean — usually discarded in production. From Ondo State farms. Tropical, slightly citrusy. The ingredient guests cannot identify but never forget.

Therapeutic notes: The thing that stays with them.

Lagos Markets · Dec–Mar

Velvet Tamarind

Known as Icheku. Every Lagos child grew up eating it from street vendors. Sour, complex, deeply nostalgic. The lead ingredient in what becomes The West.

Therapeutic notes: Digestive support. A flavour that carries memory.
